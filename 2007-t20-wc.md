# 🏏 2007 ICC World Twenty20 Summary

The **2007 ICC World Twenty20** was the inaugural championship of T20 International cricket. Hosted by **South Africa**, it transformed the shortest format of the game from an experimental exhibition into a global phenomenon.

## 🏆 The Champions: India

In a fairy-tale run, a young Indian side led by the newly appointed captain **MS Dhoni** defied all odds to become the first-ever T20 World Champions. This victory is credited with sparking the T20 revolution and the subsequent birth of the IPL.


## 📊 Tournament Overview

| Feature | Details |
| --- | --- |

| **Winner** | **India** (1st Title) |
| **Runner-up** | Pakistan |
| **Final Venue** | Wanderers Stadium, Johannesburg |


## ⚔️ The Final: India vs. Pakistan

The tournament ended with the ultimate high-stakes match: an India-Pakistan final that went down to the final over.

* **India's Innings:** India scored **157/5**, fueled by a brilliant **75 (54)** from **Gautam Gambhir**.
* **The Chase:** Pakistan struggled early but were kept alive by **Misbah-ul-Haq**, who brought them to the brink of victory.
* **The Final Over:** Pakistan needed **13 runs** from the last 6 balls. Joginder Sharma bowled the final over. After hitting a six, Misbah attempted a scoop shot with only 6 runs needed off 4 balls.
* **The Catch:** The ball looped into the air and was caught by **Sreesanth** at short fine-leg, handing India a historic **5-run victory**.


## 🌟 Iconic Moments & Awards

* **Player of the Tournament:** 🇵🇰 **Shahid Afridi** (12 wickets and vital runs).
* **Most Runs:** 🇦🇺 **Matthew Hayden** (265 runs).
* **Most Wickets:** 🇵🇰 **Umar Gul** (13 wickets).
* **The Six Sixes:** 🇮🇳 **Yuvraj Singh** famously hit **Stuart Broad** for **six sixes in a single over** against England, reaching the fastest-ever T20I half-century (12 balls).
* **The Bowl-out:** Before Super Overs existed, India defeated Pakistan in the group stage via a **bowl-out** (3-0) after the match ended in a tie.


## 🚀 Impact of the Tournament

1. **Rise of MS Dhoni:** This win established Dhoni as a tactical mastermind and "Captain Cool."
2. **Global Shift:** The success of the tournament convinced the BCCI and the world that T20 was the future, leading to the creation of the **Indian Premier League (IPL)** in 2008.
3. **New Heroes:** Players like Rohit Sharma and Suresh Raina emerged as stars during this campaign.
