# 🏏 2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup Summary

The **2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup** was the ninth edition of the tournament, held from June 1 to June 29, 2024. It was a historic event, being the first major ICC tournament to feature matches played in the **United States**, alongside co-hosts **West Indies**.

## 🏆 The Champions: India

India emerged as the champions, clinching their **second T20 World Cup title** (their first since the inaugural 2007 edition). They made history by becoming the **first team ever to win the tournament undefeated**, winning all 8 of their completed matches.

| Feature | Details |

| **Winner** | **India** (2nd Title) |
| **Runner-up** | South Africa |
| **Final Venue** | Kensington Oval, Bridgetown, Barbados |


## ⚔️ The Final: India vs. South Africa

In one of the most dramatic finals in cricket history, India defeated South Africa by **7 runs**.

* **India's Innings:** India posted **176/7**, the highest-ever total in a T20 World Cup final. **Virat Kohli** anchored the innings with a vital 76 (59).
* **South Africa's Chase:** The Proteas seemed to be cruising with Heinrich Klaasen (52 off 27) at the crease. At one point, they needed just **30 runs from 30 balls** with 6 wickets in hand.
* **The Turnaround:** A masterclass in death bowling by **Jasprit Bumrah**, **Arshdeep Singh**, and **Hardik Pandya** choked the scoring.
* **The Moment:** **Suryakumar Yadav** took a stunning, high-pressure catch on the boundary to dismiss David Miller in the final over, sealing the win for India.


## 🌟 Top Performers & Awards

* **Player of the Tournament:** 🇮🇳 **Jasprit Bumrah** (15 wickets with an incredible economy of 4.17).
* **Player of the Match (Final):** 🇮🇳 **Virat Kohli** (76 runs).
* **Most Runs:** 🇦🇫 **Rahmanullah Gurbaz** (281 runs).
* **Most Wickets:** 🇦🇫 **Fazalhaq Farooqi** & 🇮🇳 **Arshdeep Singh** (17 wickets each).


## 🚀 Key Highlights & Surprises

1. **USA's Dream Run:** The co-hosts stunned the world by defeating Pakistan in the group stage and qualifying for the Super 8s in their debut appearance.
2. **Afghanistan's Rise:** Afghanistan reached their first-ever World Cup semi-final after a historic win against Australia and a nail-biting victory over Bangladesh.
3. **End of an Era:** Following the final, three Indian legends—**Virat Kohli, Rohit Sharma, and Ravindra Jadeja**—announced their retirement from T20 International cricket.
