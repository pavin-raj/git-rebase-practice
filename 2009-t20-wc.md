# 🏏 2009 ICC World Twenty20 Summary

The **2009 ICC World Twenty20** was the second edition of the tournament, held in **England**. It is remembered for Pakistan's emotional victory, providing a moment of immense national pride during a difficult period for the country's cricket.

## 🏆 The Champions: Pakistan

After finishing as runners-up in 2007, **Pakistan** clinicaly secured their first T20 World Cup title. Their campaign was defined by a slow start followed by an unstoppable momentum in the knockout stages, led by the "Boom Boom" heroics of Shahid Afridi.


## 📊 Tournament Overview

| Feature | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Host** | England |
| **Participants** | 12 Teams |
| **Total Matches** | 27 |
| **Winner** | **Pakistan** (1st Title) |
| **Runner-up** | Sri Lanka |
| **Final Venue** | Lord's, London |


## ⚔️ The Final: Pakistan vs. Sri Lanka

The final was held at the "Home of Cricket," where Pakistan’s bowling attack dominated a previously unbeaten Sri Lankan side.

* **Sri Lanka's Innings:** Pakistan's seamers and spinners kept a tight lid on the scoring. Kumar Sangakkara fought a lone battle with **64***, guiding Sri Lanka to a modest **138/6**.
* **The Chase:** Pakistan chased the target with ease, reaching **139/2** with 8 balls to spare.
* **Afridi’s Masterclass:** **Shahid Afridi** proved his big-game temperament, scoring an unbeaten **54 (40)** and taking a crucial wicket (1/20), earning him the Player of the Match.


## 🌟 Top Performers & Awards

* **Player of the Tournament:** 🇱🇰 **Tillakaratne Dilshan** (317 runs). He popularized the "Dil-scoop" during this event.
* **Most Runs:** 🇱🇰 **Tillakaratne Dilshan** (317 runs).
* **Most Wickets:** 🇵🇰 **Umar Gul** (13 wickets). He became the first bowler to take a five-wicket haul in T20Is (5/6 vs. New Zealand).
* **Impact Player:** 🇵🇰 **Shahid Afridi** (Match-winning fifties in both the Semi-final and the Final).

## 🚀 Key Highlights

1. **Dutch Delight:** In the opening match, associate nation **Netherlands** stunned hosts **England** in a last-ball thriller at Lord's.
2. **Defending Champs Out:** India, the 2007 winners, failed to win a single match in the Super 8 stage and were knocked out early.
3. **The "Dil-scoop":** Tillakaratne Dilshan revolutionized batting by consistently hitting the ball over the wicket-keeper’s head.
4. **Women’s Inclusion:** This tournament was held alongside the **Women's World Twenty20**, with England winning the inaugural women's title.
